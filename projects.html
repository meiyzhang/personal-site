Quantum and Classical Portfolio Optimization: Continuous Allocation and Discrete Selection via QAOA

[ quantum computing · finance · June 2026 ]

Project testing the limitations and opportunities of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) in an eleven-asset ETF universe, solving the discrete selection problem. The usage of QAOA is benchmarked against the classical solution to the continuous allocation problem and simulated annealing to test the confidence of quantum algorithmic usage within current physical constraints.
