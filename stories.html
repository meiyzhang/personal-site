interconnected.

recently came across a substack article on finding unexpected love -- and making sure you don't stick to a determination without leaving room for exploration. i thought about a recent read of mine by mitch albom, how it had touched on how human connections and stories are always interconnected in strange, unexpected, but nevertheless, heartwarming ways. in reflecting on this, i've been lending a lot of more grace and attention to seeing things in relation to others: place and feeling, poetry and semantics, form and colour. My recent way of seeing the world, thanks to some few strangers on the internet :)

how certain are we?

lately, i've been getting my head up with studying financial formulas, following market trends, and listening to podcasts reporting on the tech boom. all of these things have echoed a feeling of needing to be certain -- a sort of rush to get the numbers right and the hypotheses logically airtight before you pitch it. but, in a world that actually runs on probabilities, how certain are we really?
